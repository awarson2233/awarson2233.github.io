Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Personal Drive Backup is used solely by its owner to upload the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive via rclone.

  • The app accesses Google Drive only to upload, list and manage the backup files placed there by the owner.
  • No personal data or Google user data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold, or shared with any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own server and are never transmitted anywhere other than Google.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

隐私政策

本应用仅供所有者本人使用，通过 rclone 将本人的文件上传到本人的 Google Drive。应用只访问 Google Drive 中的备份文件，不收集、不出售、不向任何第三方共享任何个人数据或 Google 用户数据。OAuth 令牌只保存在所有者自己的服务器上，除 Google 外不会发送给任何一方。可随时在 Google 账号的「第三方应用访问权限」页面撤销授权。
